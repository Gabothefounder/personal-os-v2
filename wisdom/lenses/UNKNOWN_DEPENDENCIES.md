# Lens: Unknown dependencies

Status: active (definition only)

This lens does not come from a single thinker. Load it when an intervention, product, policy, or “simplification” might remove something whose function is not yet understood.

## Questions

- What appears minor or invisible but may be structurally necessary?
- What second- and third-order effects could our intervention cause?
- What capability, relationship, norm, habit or institution could disappear before we realize what function it performed?

## Use

Name the dependency, the function it might perform, and what we would notice only after it was gone.

Do not fill gaps with confident causal stories. If the function is unknown, label it UNKNOWN.
