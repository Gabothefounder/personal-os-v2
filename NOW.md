# NOW

## Primary project

ScanScam

## 30-day objective

Determine whether ScanScam can generate paid revenue by helping people or businesses evaluate what deserves their trust before committing money, reputation, or significant time.

## Primary business hypothesis

Businesses may pay for independent research that reduces uncertainty about an unfamiliar company, supplier, distributor, partner, or counterparty.

This is a hypothesis, not a validated business.

## Current experiment

Test a manually delivered "Scan a Company / Counterparty Scan."

Do not build software for it yet.

## Initial success criteria

Within 30 days:

- speak with at least 10 relevant people
- present the offer to real prospects
- complete at least 3 pilot analyses if demand exists
- obtain at least 1 paid engagement
- document why people buy, refuse, hesitate, or do not care

Revenue matters, but learning whether trust/uncertainty is actually the buying problem matters equally.

## This week's objective

Get the offer in front of real people.

## Highest-leverage actions

1. Define the smallest Counterparty Scan deliverable.
2. Choose the first customer profile.
3. Create one simple offer.
4. Contact prospects.
5. Publish one piece of content that tests the problem.
6. Record objections and customer language.

## Do not build yet

- trust platform
- agent marketplace
- trust passport
- automated company-analysis engine
- complex OSINT infrastructure
- new brand
- new application
- agent swarm

## Current intellectual context

The broader Trust Thesis explores how humans, organizations, institutions, and machines cooperate under uncertainty.

ScanScam is currently the real-world laboratory for testing applications of that thesis.
