# Naval Lens

Status: active (ingestion #1). Deep notes: `wisdom/people/NAVAL_RAVIKANT.md`

Load this file, not the deep library. Empty adjacent lenses stay empty—do not impersonate Taleb or Hormozi here.

This is **not** a score. If tensions conflict, write both.

---

## 1. Game

Is this a game worth playing for a long time, with people who will still be here?

Does entering it **reset** accumulated reputation, relationships, or knowledge? (He says switching industries often means starting over—*even if* the new opportunity is “best.”)

Is it **positive-sum pie-baking** or a one-move pie-cut / status hierarchy?

Long-term players make each other rich; short-term players make themselves rich. Convert one-shots into repeated, reputation-bearing games when leverage is poor.

## 2. Specific knowledge

What part pays for knowledge that **cannot be class-trained** (or now: cannot be agent-stamped)?

Is it tied to **genuine curiosity / observed talent**, or to the hottest job?

If AI commoditizes the teachable layer, what remains that is still *this person, this situation, this obsession*?

## 3. Ownership

What do we **own** if this works—equity, IP, a product, a name, residuals?

Renting time (inputs ≈ outputs) is not his wealth path. Accountability is how equity shows up: neck out, replaceable people get salary.

## 4. Leverage

Can code, media, capital, agents, labor, or distribution scale results **without** scaling hours 1:1?

Prefer **permissionless** leverage (code/media) when you lack permission. Labor is messy; use the minimum needed to wield the other levers.

2026 overlay: agents are cheap labor/agency; **tokens are cheaper than human time**—but average output has no demand.

## 5. Judgment

Once leverage exists, you are steering a tanker. A slightly better long-term call dominates hustle.

Where can **one** bad judgment, integrity failure, or bet-the-kitty wipe the upside (ruin = jail, body, capital, name)?

Intellect without skin in the game is often worse than useless. Outrage and chronic emotion degrade judgment.

## 6. Compounding

What actually compounds if we stay—knowledge, relationships, brand, capital, distribution, data, reputation, trust?

Most compounding payoff is **at the end**. Counting the years too early is how you quit.

If we leave, what compounding do we throw away?

## 7. Build + sell

Can we **build** the value in this industry’s real sense?

Can we **sell the truth**—credibility, no attachment to closing, walk away if it doesn’t resonate—or does the plan require misrepresentation / guru-dynamics / “founder lies to employees”?

If selling *feels* like selling, he says you’re probably selling the wrong thing.

## 8. Future

Does this put us **inside** an important future (avid use of the new tools; building, not punditry) or merely automate yesterday’s workflow?

Operational “live in the future”: consume and use the frontier so you can recognize it. Feb 2026: vibe-code/specify; models adapt faster than prompt-trick study.

Would we still be **#1 in a niche** when there is no demand for average?

## 9. Freedom

If it works, does it increase or reduce freedom of **time, location, relationships, capital, attention**?

Wealth’s job is sovereignty—not a nicer cage. Lifestyle creep and “heroin and a monthly salary” recapture you.

Busy calendar / busy mind destroy the work that creates freedom.

## 10. Peace / good life

Would success be a life worth having—or chronic agitation, status combat, and work that **destroys decision quality**?

Desire is a contract to be unhappy until you get the thing. A fit body, calm mind, and house of love cannot be bought.

He admits the tradeoff: maximum peace is in tension with Musk-scale building. Do not pretend the lens resolves that.

## Kill questions

- If this succeeds, **what actually compounds**?
- Why am **I** unusually suited to this (observed, not wished)?
- **What do I own?**
- Long-term game—or temporary arbitrage that resets the network?
- Is this **positive-sum**?
- Does AI make this **stronger** or **erase** it (average / wrap-the-model)?
- Would I still want these **people and this industry in 10 years**?
- Does success buy **freedom** or another job?
- Is **desire** for the outcome distorting judgment (including in the negotiation)?
- Can we sell it **without lying**?
- What’s the **ruin** path?
- Are we playing a **stupid game** because competitors are standing there?
