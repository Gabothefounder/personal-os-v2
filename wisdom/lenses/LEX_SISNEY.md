# Lex Sisney Lens

Status: active (ingestion #1)
Deep notes: `wisdom/people/LEX_SISNEY.md`
Book notes: `wisdom/books/ORGANIZATIONAL_PHYSICS.md`

Use this lens for organizational design, scaling, AI delegation, workflow structure, stage-fit, and internal friction.

Do not treat the physics metaphor as literal scientific law.

---

## 1. System

What system behavior is producing the symptom?

Do not fix a person, tool, or process before checking the surrounding structure and incentives.

## 2. Stage

What stage are we actually in?

Do not build a scaling structure for a discovery-stage problem.

## 3. Integration

Where is the real external opportunity, demand, or useful environmental signal?

Internal elegance without environmental fit is failure.

## 4. Entropy

What is consuming energy without creating enough external value?

Look for rework, coordination drag, decision delay, duplicated tooling, unclear ownership, stale rules, and maintenance burden.

Do not confuse all slack or exploration with waste.

## 5. PSIU

What force is missing or excessive?

- **P** — Produce a result now
- **S** — Stabilize what already works
- **I** — Innovate / adapt to change
- **U** — Unify people and context

Do not Stabilize before fit. Do not Innovate forever without Producing.

## 6. Structure

Does structure match strategy and stage?

Keep long-range work from being swallowed by short-range execution. Keep efficiency from controlling effectiveness. Balance local autonomy with central accountability.

## 7. Flow

Where is the end-to-end flow broken?

Customer journey, product development, strategic execution, and employee/agent experience cut across functions.

**Terminology warning:** Sisney's "horizontal" means cross-functional end-to-end flow; it does not automatically mean broad market exploration.

## 8. Accountability

Who owns the outcome?

Important work needs one clear accountable owner even when sensemaking is shared.

## 9. AI delegation

Use:

> **Design → Delegate → Observe → Redesign**

Specify outcome, quality, constraints, safeguards, and feedback before delegating.

Keep high-impact / hard-to-reverse decisions human. Delegate reversible work as controls and evidence improve.

## 10. Context

What proprietary context makes the system better than a generic model?

Do not confuse a brittle wrapper around today's model weakness with durable advantage.

## 11. Sensemaking

Automate routing and craft where useful; do not casually delegate the shared interpretation of ambiguous reality.

## 12. Scaling

Standardize only what reality has repeated.

Institution follows demonstrated utility, not architectural enthusiasm.

---

## Kill questions

- Are we solving the system or patching the symptom?
- What stage are we actually in?
- Where is the external integration signal?
- What is the biggest source of entropy?
- Are we standardizing before something has earned stability?
- Is one PSIU force dominating the wrong stage?
- Does the structure make the important flow easier or harder?
- Who owns the outcome?
- Is this delegation reversible and checkable?
- Does this AI layer deepen proprietary context or merely wrap the model?
- Are rules multiplying faster than useful work?
- What should be redesigned after observing reality?
