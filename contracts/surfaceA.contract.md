Surface A Contract — Canonical Substrate
Version: v1
 Status: Frozen
 Scope: Personal OS v2

1. Purpose
Surface A produces the Canonical Substrate: a daily, internal, non-user-facing synthesis of recent reality.
Its job is to:
compress recent inputs into a bounded, reliable internal state


preserve truth without interpretation


fail visibly rather than substitute or guess


Surface A is infrastructure, not experience.

2. Non-Goals (Explicit)
Surface A does not:
decide importance or urgency


assign tasks


infer emotion, intent, or identity


coach, advise, or motivate


smooth tone or language


ask questions or request confirmation


perform memory consolidation across long history


Any of the above belongs to downstream surfaces.

3. Authoritative Inputs
Surface A may read only the following inputs.
3.1 RAW
Verbatim human capture (voice → text)


Append-only


Immutable


Event-based


Surface A reads:
RAW entries since the last successful Surface A run only


Surface A must never reread full historical RAW.

3.2 DERIVED (Open States Only)
Auto-created


Provisional


Reversible


Descriptive only


DERIVED represents:
conditions that appear to still be true


unresolved situations


ongoing states


DERIVED entries:
may be wrong


require no confirmation


never create obligations


remain open until explicitly closed


Surface A reads:
DERIVED entries marked open



3.3 DECIDED (Read-Only)
Human-confirmed only


Authoritative


Irreversible without explicit reversal


DECIDED entries:
close DERIVED states


escalate importance


create commitments


establish principles


Surface A may read DECIDED entries only insofar as they affect current open state.
 Surface A must never generate DECIDED content.

4. Output — Canonical Substrate
Surface A outputs one daily canonical substrate with the following fixed shape:
orientation — concrete, recognizable signals


attention — neutral directional note


context — situational framing


framing — overall shape of the day


Output requirements:
bounded length


structurally complete


neutral language


no coaching, tone, or emotional framing


derived only from declared inputs


The Canonical Substrate is not prose and not narrative.

5. Processing Rules
Surface A is incremental


Surface A operates on bounded input


Surface A must not rely on implicit memory


Surface A must not infer importance from verbosity


Surface A must not privilege reflective or journal content


Operational facts and open states take precedence over reflection.

6. Failure Semantics (Mandatory)
Surface A must fail hard and visibly if:
output is incomplete


structure is invalid


input exceeds safe bounds


synthesis cannot complete deterministically


On failure:
no fallback content is written


no partial output is persisted


previous successful substrate remains unchanged


failure is explicitly logged


Silence or failure is preferable to false certainty.

7. Automation Rules
Reversible things may be auto-created


Irreversible things require human action


Therefore:
DERIVED may be auto-created


DECIDED must never be auto-created


Closing, escalation, or commitment is always manual



8. Surface Boundaries
Surface A:
produces the Canonical Substrate


never asks for confirmation


never interacts with the user


never shapes tone or voice


Surface B and other lenses:
read Surface A output


add tone, voice, and framing


must not mutate the canonical substrate



9. Change Control
This contract is frozen.
Any change requires:
explicit reopening of the contract


acknowledgment of downstream impact


deliberate architectural review



10. Design Doctrine (Summary)
Truth over completeness


Structure over cleverness


Reversibility before automation


Human authority where it matters


Failure is a feature, not a bug
