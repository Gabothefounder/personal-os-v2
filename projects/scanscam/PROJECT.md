# ScanScam

## Mission

Help people make better decisions about what deserves their trust.

## Current wedge

ScanScam began with suspicious-message / scam analysis.

## Current strategic question

Can ScanScam expand from detecting suspicious messages into helping people and businesses evaluate higher-value trust decisions?

## 30-day business question

Will someone pay ScanScam to reduce uncertainty before doing business with an unfamiliar company or counterparty?

## Initial customer hypothesis

Small and medium-sized businesses considering:

- suppliers
- distributors
- overseas partners
- contractors
- service providers
- other unfamiliar counterparties

Customer segment is not validated.

## Initial offer hypothesis

"Scan a Company / Counterparty Scan"

A human-reviewed, public-source research brief designed to identify:

- identity
- corporate existence
- important claims
- management / ownership information where available
- reputation
- public history
- inconsistencies
- obvious red flags
- evidence gaps
- questions that should be answered before proceeding

The service should clearly distinguish:

VERIFIED
SUPPORTED
UNCERTAIN
CONTRADICTED
UNKNOWN

It must not promise that a company is "safe" or "trustworthy."

## Value proposition hypothesis

Reduce uncertainty before the customer commits money, reputation, or significant time.

## Delivery model

Initially manual.

Use AI and research tools internally for leverage, but human review remains part of the pilot.

Do not automate until repeated manual work reveals what should be automated.

## Pricing hypothesis

To be tested.

Do not lock pricing yet.

Potential pilot range: approximately $500–$1,500 depending on scope.

## Evidence required

We need evidence that:

1. this problem occurs frequently enough
2. uncertainty actually delays or kills transactions
3. customers already spend money/time trying to solve it
4. existing alternatives are inadequate
5. customers will pay ScanScam
6. we can produce useful information responsibly
7. the service can eventually produce attractive margins

## Major risks

- trust may not be the actual purchasing problem
- existing due-diligence providers may already solve it
- customers may expect guarantees we cannot provide
- public information may be insufficient
- legal/compliance boundaries may constrain the service
- generic AI may commoditize basic company analysis
- customer acquisition may be expensive
- one-off research may not create recurring revenue

## Strategic upside if validated

The initial service could expose more valuable opportunities involving:

- counterparty intelligence
- international commerce
- claims verification
- transaction risk
- agent-to-agent trust
- provenance
- reputation
- verification infrastructure

These are possibilities, not roadmap commitments.

## Current stage

DISCOVERY

## Next milestone

Obtain real customer evidence before building additional product.
