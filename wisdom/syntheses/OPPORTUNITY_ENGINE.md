## Core opportunity loop

> **SEARCH → ASSUMPTIONS → FORECAST → RED TEAM → TEST → UPDATE**

This should stay simple.

### SEARCH
Search broadly before committing. Look for consequential problems, triggering moments, payers, existing spend, changing technology/regulation, and reachable channels.

### ASSUMPTIONS
Ask what must be true for the opportunity to work. Keep four risks in view:

- **want** — do people care enough to act?
- **deliver** — can we actually produce the promised outcome?
- **economics** — can acquisition + delivery produce a worthwhile business?
- **survive** — can it remain useful against competition, regulation, platform risk, and improving AI?

Do not build a giant assumption register. Identify the few high-impact unknowns with weak evidence.

### FORECAST
Before seeing the result, state what you expect numerically when possible.

Examples:

- probability of at least one paid pilot from 20 qualified prospects
- percentage of targeted visitors expected to begin a real case
- expected acquisition cost range
- probability a manual case can be delivered inside the promised time/cost

Use `wisdom/lenses/SUPERFORECASTING.md` for consequential uncertainty.

### RED TEAM
Before committing, deliberately search for why the conclusion could be wrong.

At minimum:

- strongest counterargument
- relevant base rate / outside view
- disconfirming evidence
- pre-mortem: assume failure; explain why
- misleading signal risk

Red-team the conclusion, not merely the execution plan.

### TEST
Choose the cheapest credible evidence that can change the important belief.

Calls are one test, not a mandatory ritual. Landing pages, mock sales, ads, deposits, concierge delivery, technical spikes, data checks, partner tests, and customer conversations answer different questions.

Prefer **pull-triggered conversations** when possible: talk to people who clicked, replied, submitted, booked, paid, or otherwise showed signal. Use cold conversations when they are the cheapest credible way to resolve an important unknown.

### UPDATE
Record what happened and change the belief.

Do not reinterpret the original prediction after seeing the result.

Decision: **WATCH / TEST / BUILD / KILL**.

## Existing-project gravity

Before recommending a new project, ask whether the opportunity can be tested through an existing active project, audience, relationship, asset, product, reputation, distribution channel, or data set.

A new project resets attention, network, learning, distribution, tooling, and compounding.

This does **not** mean force every opportunity into ScanScam. Make the reset cost explicit, then choose.

## Opportunity flow

1. Capture in `opportunities/INBOX.md`
2. Broad research if needed
3. Move only serious candidates to `opportunities/ACTIVE.md`
4. Keep one analysis in `opportunities/analyses/`
5. Run the core loop
6. Record outcome; archive when done
7. Update `GABRIEL.md` only from real outcomes or explicit preferences

Keep few active opportunities at once.
