# Franklin Lens

Status: active (ingestion #1). Deep notes: `wisdom/people/BENJAMIN_FRANKLIN.md`

Load this file, not the deep library. Empty adjacent lenses stay empty.

This is **not** a score. If tensions conflict, write both. Franklin often did.

He is a printer-experimenter who turns a local defect into a paper, a club, a subscription, a copyable company, and sometimes a law—while managing credit, hiding or claiming authorship as the situation requires, and recording **errata**. Do not flatten him into maxims.

---

## 1. Utility

What concrete human problem does this solve—mud in the street, fire, unread books, unpaid sick, a counterparty who may not pay?

Cleverness without a user is a whistle. “Human felicity is produced not so much by great pieces of good fortune that seldom happen, as by little advantages that occur every day.”

If you cannot name the repeating nuisance, you are not in this lens yet.

## 2. Experiment

What inexpensive real-world test can produce information **now**?

Franklin’s stack: Junto query → short paper → one company / one block / one subscription list → copy if it works.

Hospital matching, sixpence sweepers, Whitefield’s hearing-distance walk: measure before you mythologize.

A three-or-four-day pro/con sheet (Priestley, 1772) is the decision experiment when the costs are not small.

## 3. Practicality

Can this be done with people, money, and tools already in reach?

Yearly quotas so the subscription can be larger. Articles of partnership while esteem is high. Conditional clauses the other side will vote for because they think they will not pay.

If it requires a governor’s unwritten letters of credit, stop.

## 4. Reputation

What credit does this accumulate or destroy?

Credit is money; the good paymaster commands other people’s purse. Be industrious **in fact** and avoid appearances to the contrary (wheelbarrow).

Also: hide authorship when your name taxes the idea; **name Bond and Clifton** when the idea was theirs; restore creditors in full when you can (Denham).

Reputation management and authenticity are both in him. Do not pick one and call it Franklin.

## 5. Network

Which useful relationships and reciprocal obligations does it create?

Junto: inquiry without victory-debating; also jobs, subscribers, and political hands.

Fix obligation on earth, not in heaven. Gifts that “compound relationships” can still be oratory emptying a pocket against a prior plan.

## 6. Institution

If this works repeatedly, should it become a durable system rather than remain an ad hoc fix?

Fire company → more companies. Library with down payment + annual. Academy with five-year quotas. Do not freeze a creed in print if you expect further light (Dunkers)—and still write partner duties explicitly.

Survival of the institution is a large part of his long-term reputation. Survival is not the same as justice.

## 7. Distribution

How will the idea, product, or knowledge circulate **without one person pushing every instance**?

Print: pamphlet, gazette, almanac, German translation, pens in the hall. Poor Richard is a distribution machine **and** a commercial persona.

When you later hold the post-office, do not forbid the other man’s papers the way Bradford did. Underdog bribes of riders are not the same principle.

*Litera scripta manet*—what you publish is the attack surface.

## 8. Independence

Does success increase future freedom and optionality?

Industry and frugality were means to a “sufficient tho’ moderate fortune” and scientific leisure. The public then laid hold of the man of leisure.

Never ask, never refuse, never resign an office—this preserves optionality **and** can become attachment to spoils.

Keith’s empty patronage is fake optionality. Verify the instrument.

## 9. Discipline

What repeated behavior is required?

One virtue at a time; daily marks; temperance first for a clear head. Order may stay failed; the attempt can still mend the hand. The “speckled ax” rationalization is a known failure mode.

Curiosity (books, sermons, inventions) will debauch the schedule. He knew it.

Resolution lasts until Whitefield’s voice. Do not pretend the chart beats oratory.

## 10. Public Benefit

Does this improve the surrounding ecosystem or merely extract from it?

Stove: decline the patent; London copyist takes the rent anyway. Library: tradesmen as readers. Hospital: poor sick.

Also: he printed the paper money he had argued for—a profitable job. Civic argument and capture of the contract can be the same afternoon.

Leave the city better (pavement, lamps, post). Do not claim the origin if it was someone else’s.

## 11. Diplomacy

Where do parties overlap enough to cooperate without agreeing on everything?

Quakers need not vote for war if they can be absent from a lottery. Associators sign when the Assembly will not. Matching grants give opponents the credit of charity.

Do not publicly dispute a professional on his turf if you still need the wagons—**do** log the forecast and refuse the premature firework.

Madeira, fasts, and printed forms are closing tools. They sit next to “no hurtful deceit.” Write the tension; do not average it.

## 12. Revision

What evidence would make us change the plan?

Errata named: indenture, Vernon’s money, Deborah Read, Order never acquired, humility as appearance, paper money later seen as having “limits.”

Pride peeps out even if you are proud of your humility.

If the experiment is rhetoric (a sermon, a room, a brand), ask whether it overrode a prior resolution in minutes.

## Kill questions

- Is it actually useful or merely clever?
- What can we test cheaply this week?
- What reputation are we accumulating?
- Who benefits enough to cooperate?
- What relationships compound if this works?
- Are we creating independence or another dependency?
- Is this a recurring problem that deserves an institution?
- Can the idea spread without Gabriel personally pushing every instance?
- What would Franklin measure or observe before committing further?
- Is compromise creating cooperation or destroying principle?
- Does this leave the system better?
