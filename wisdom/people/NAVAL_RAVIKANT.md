## 1. Wealth vs money vs status

### NAVAL-001
PRINCIPLE: Seek wealth, not money or status.
SOURCE: Seek Wealth, Not Money or Status — https://nav.al/seek-wealth (also https://nav.al/rich)
SOURCE CLAIM: Wealth is assets that earn while you sleep (businesses, code, invested capital). Money is how we transfer wealth (social IOUs). Status is rank in a social hierarchy.
CONTEXT: Opening of the How to Get Rich interviews; people confuse the three games.
APPLICATION: Diagnose whether an opportunity creates assets, collects IOUs, or bids for rank.
BOUNDARY CONDITIONS: Status still allocates who is in charge; he calls status games a “necessary evil.”
TENSION: NAVAL-054 (peace/health/love cannot be bought) vs wealth-as-priority.
COUNTERARGUMENT: Many high-leverage goods (attention, political power, institutional access) still run on status, not sleep-earning assets.

### NAVAL-002
PRINCIPLE: The purpose of wealth is freedom, not consumption theater.
SOURCE: https://nav.al/seek-wealth
SOURCE CLAIM: You want wealth so you are a sovereign individual—not ties, commutes, or soulless jobs. Yachts and jets get “boring and stupid, really fast.”
CONTEXT: Why bother getting rich.
APPLICATION: If success would increase schedule captivity, it failed his wealth test.
BOUNDARY CONDITIONS: Competitive elements remain because resources are finite *right now*.
TENSION: NAVAL-032 (wealth must be overwhelming priority) vs NAVAL-054.
COUNTERARGUMENT: Consumption and signaling often *are* how markets allocate mates, talent, and trust.

### NAVAL-003
PRINCIPLE: Wealth creation is (mostly) positive-sum; status is zero-sum.
SOURCE: https://nav.al/seek-wealth
SOURCE CLAIM: Houses don’t subtract houses; status slots do. Creating abundance is not taking. Wealth creators will be attacked by status players.
CONTEXT: Moral attack on making money.
APPLICATION: Prefer games where others getting rich doesn’t require someone losing rank.
BOUNDARY CONDITIONS: He admits competitive elements and “makers vs takers” (theft, taxes, cronyism).
TENSION: Winner-take-all network effects (NAVAL-028) look zero-sum among competitors.
COUNTERARGUMENT: Land, attention, spectrum, and positional goods are scarce; “everyone can be rich” is historically relative, not absolute.

### NAVAL-004
PRINCIPLE: Ethical wealth is creating what society wants and doesn’t yet know how to get—at scale.
SOURCE: Give Society What It Doesn’t Know How to Get — https://nav.al/rich
SOURCE CLAIM: Society pays IOUs for new things. If it already knew how, it wouldn’t need you. Scale from one to millions.
CONTEXT: What to work on.
APPLICATION: Discount “same thing, slightly cheaper” unless scale/leverage is new.
BOUNDARY CONDITIONS: Starts as creativity for yourself, then rich people, then mass market (Jobs/Uber pattern).
TENSION: Authenticity (NAVAL-011) vs what society will actually pay for.
COUNTERARGUMENT: Many fortunes come from rent-seeking, regulatory capture, or copying, not invention.

## 2. Specific knowledge

### NAVAL-005
PRINCIPLE: Specific knowledge can’t be trained like a class; if it can, it will be mass-produced or automated.
SOURCE: Arm Yourself With Specific Knowledge — https://nav.al/specific-knowledge (2019)
SOURCE CLAIM: The interesting things cannot be taught, but everything can be learned. Trainable skills devolve toward training cost + ROI; computers/robots follow.
CONTEXT: Why you get paid vs someone else.
APPLICATION: Ask: could a bootcamp or an agent stamp this out?
BOUNDARY CONDITIONS: Schooling still helps you *capitalize* on specific knowledge.
TENSION: 2026 claim that English + agents let non-coders ship apps (NAVAL-066) looks like training-by-model.
COUNTERARGUMENT: Apprenticeship and deliberate practice *do* transfer “unteachable” crafts; he later relies on iteration + skin in the game (NAVAL-030).

### NAVAL-006
PRINCIPLE: Find specific knowledge via genuine curiosity, innate talent, and observation—not the hottest job.
SOURCE: https://nav.al/specific-knowledge
SOURCE CLAIM: Psychology won’t make a non-salesperson great at sales. Look backward at what felt like play and what others already saw (his mother: business, not astrophysics).
CONTEXT: People trying to pick a lucrative major.
APPLICATION: Don’t assemble a career purely from market heat.
BOUNDARY CONDITIONS: You can keep commercial aspects “in the back of your mind” without making money the selector.
TENSION: NAVAL-004 (give society what it wants) vs curiosity-first.
COUNTERARGUMENT: Markets pay for trained scarcity (pilots, surgeons) that *is* teachable; curiosity can be a luxury.

### NAVAL-007
PRINCIPLE: Specific knowledge is individual, situational, and built by obsession at the edge—not one book or course.
SOURCE: Specific Knowledge Is Highly Creative or Technical — https://nav.al/rich
SOURCE CLAIM: Taught via apprenticeship/on the job. Creative or technical, bleeding edge of tech/art/communication. Combinations beat being #1 at one thing, but don’t assemble too deliberately.
CONTEXT: Scott Adams’ skill-stack vs Naval’s “follow obsession.”
APPLICATION: Stack skills you are a natural at; don’t fake a stack for money.
BOUNDARY CONDITIONS: Combinatorics help because #1 slots are taken.
TENSION: NAVAL-033 (become #1; keep redefining) vs not being too goal-oriented on money.
COUNTERARGUMENT: Many specialists win without “play”; obsession is survivorship-biased.

## 3. Authenticity / genuine curiosity

### NAVAL-008
PRINCIPLE: Escape competition through authenticity.
SOURCE: Escape Competition Through Authenticity — https://nav.al/rich; also internet-careers section
SOURCE CLAIM: You copy → you compete. No one competes with you on being you. Post-internet, authenticity can be a career. Pre-internet this advice was often useless.
CONTEXT: Girard/mimesis; Thiel on competition.
APPLICATION: If the plan is “like X but us,” expect a status/competition trap.
BOUNDARY CONDITIONS: Too authentic with no product-market fit (best unicycle juggler) still fails. Empty markets can be a warning.
TENSION: NAVAL-004; NAVAL-027 (winner-take-all rage at competitors).
COUNTERARGUMENT: Platforms and categories force copycats; “be yourself” is not a strategy in commodities.

### NAVAL-009
PRINCIPLE: Motivation has to be intrinsic; you cannot sell someone into long-term work.
SOURCE: Pick Partners… — https://nav.al/rich
SOURCE CLAIM: You can inspire briefly; you cannot be their crutch. Partner with people already into the thing.
CONTEXT: Hiring and co-founders.
APPLICATION: Don’t staff a ScanScam/commerce bet with people who need a pep talk every week.
BOUNDARY CONDITIONS: Kings can get a charge into battle; companies need years.
TENSION: Leadership as making people *want* to do it (NAVAL-040).
COUNTERARGUMENT: Compensation, culture, and management routinely create motivation he treats as fake.

## 4. Ownership / equity

### NAVAL-010
PRINCIPLE: You will not get rich renting out your time; you must own equity in a product, business, or IP.
SOURCE: You Won’t Get Rich Renting Out Your Time — https://nav.al/rich (related: https://nav.al/renting-time)
SOURCE CLAIM: Salary ties inputs to outputs; sleep/vacation/retirement earn nothing. Even rich doctors got rich via practice/IP/brand. Owners keep surplus; renters get the minimum to show up.
CONTEXT: How people mistakenly think “work harder at a job.”
APPLICATION: Map who owns the residual claim.
BOUNDARY CONDITIONS: Stock options at a tech company are “a fine way to start.” He also says you can apply accountability *inside* a job without quitting (Q&A).
TENSION: NAVAL-032 (hard work) vs ownership as the actual mechanism.
COUNTERARGUMENT: High-skill wages, carried interest at funds, and professional partnerships *are* wealth for many; “must found” is Silicon Valley local.

### NAVAL-011
PRINCIPLE: Accountability is how you get equity: less replaceable → upside, more downside.
SOURCE: Take Accountability to Earn Equity — https://nav.al/rich
SOURCE CLAIM: Equity is paid after salaries and debt. Taking accountability is like taking equity in your work. Modern failure (if honest) is more forgivable than integrity failure (Madoff ruins the name).
CONTEXT: Why to put your name on things.
APPLICATION: Clear owners per slice of work; avoid unowned committees.
BOUNDARY CONDITIONS: Airplane-captain accountability can be literally fatal; he distinguishes business context.
TENSION: Freedom (NAVAL-002) vs accountability (tall poppy, fame downsides).
COUNTERARGUMENT: Many extract equity via politics/inheritance without personal accountability.

## 5. Leverage

### NAVAL-012
PRINCIPLE: Fortunes require leverage: labor, capital, or products with no marginal cost of replication.
SOURCE: Labor and Capital Are Old Leverage / Product and Media Are New Leverage — https://nav.al/rich
SOURCE CLAIM: Labor is oldest and overvalued (headcount as status). Capital dominated last century. Code/media are permissionless and make new billionaires. Combine minimum high-output labor + capital + code/media.
CONTEXT: Archimedes tweet; “how many people work there?”
APPLICATION: Classify the leverage. Prefer permissionless when starting without permission.
BOUNDARY CONDITIONS: Labor still needed as the minimum to use other levers; capital still converts to other things.
TENSION: Labor leverage is “messy/mutiny” vs needing a hunt-band (NAVAL-041).
COUNTERARGUMENT: Most of the real economy still runs on labor-heavy services; code leverage is not universal.

### NAVAL-013
PRINCIPLE: New leverage is permissionless; labor and capital usually are not.
SOURCE: https://nav.al/rich (Product and Media)
SOURCE CLAIM: Coding, podcasts, tweets, YouTube don’t require someone to follow you or give you money first. That is why they are egalitarian equalizers.
CONTEXT: Why learn to code (2019 framing: commands to robot armies in datacenters).
APPLICATION: Can we start without a gatekeeper?
BOUNDARY CONDITIONS: He later notes capital/labor becoming more permissionless via crowdfunding and community.
TENSION: 2026: aggregators still take “super wealth” (NAVAL-067).
COUNTERARGUMENT: Discovery, app stores, and model APIs *are* permissioned.

### NAVAL-014
PRINCIPLE: Inputs should not match outputs.
SOURCE: https://nav.al/rich (renting time / leverage)
SOURCE CLAIM: Creative + leveraged work: one hour can be huge or a thousand hours worthless (software). Lumberjack without tools: maybe 3x spread.
CONTEXT: Choosing a profession.
APPLICATION: If hours≈pay, wealth creation is capped.
BOUNDARY CONDITIONS: Tools can add leverage even to “old” jobs (bulldozer lumberjack).
TENSION: Inspiration sprints (NAVAL-034) still look like hours.
COUNTERARGUMENT: In many crafts, hours *are* the quality function.

## 6. Judgment

### NAVAL-015
PRINCIPLE: In an age of nearly infinite leverage, judgment is the decisive skill.
SOURCE: Judgment Is the Decisive Skill — https://nav.al/judgment and https://nav.al/rich
SOURCE CLAIM: First hustle to get leverage; then slow down—you’re steering a tanker. 10–20% better judgment on a huge ship dwarfs CEO pay. Buffett: money gone, capital would still show up.
CONTEXT: After specific knowledge, accountability, leverage.
APPLICATION: As agents/code scale, *what you choose* matters more than effort.
BOUNDARY CONDITIONS: Without demonstrated judgment + integrity, nobody hands you the tanker.
TENSION: Action bias (NAVAL-019) vs slow-down-after-leverage.
COUNTERARGUMENT: Many fortunes are distribution/timing, not “wiser judgment.”

### NAVAL-016
PRINCIPLE: Judgment is knowing long-term consequences; intellect without experience is often worse than useless.
SOURCE: https://nav.al/judgment
SOURCE CLAIM: Wisdom ≈ personal judgment. Ivory-tower intellect without skin in the game (Taleb) gives false confidence. Prefer 10,000 *tries*. Least emotional people often have best judgment. Outrage on Twitter ≈ worse judgment.
CONTEXT: Why investors read philosophy/history not “investment books.”
APPLICATION: Distrust high-certainty plans from people with no downside.
BOUNDARY CONDITIONS: You still need intellect; experience without models also fails.
TENSION: Desire/peace reducing drive (NAVAL-055) vs needing clear seeing.
COUNTERARGUMENT: Emotions carry information; “robotic investor” is a finance subculture.

### NAVAL-017
PRINCIPLE: Adding more estimated variables to a decision makes the answer worse; pick the one or two biggest factors.
SOURCE: Eventually You Will Get What You Deserve — https://nav.al/rich
SOURCE CLAIM: False precision. Nivi’s multiplicative “equation” is a sketch, not math. Contradictory-sounding maxims are contextual, not algebra. Better: what am I good at that the market values?
CONTEXT: People turning philosophy into formulas.
APPLICATION: No “Naval score.” Two questions beat a 12-factor model.
BOUNDARY CONDITIONS: The longer equation is still a useful checklist if not multiplied as if measured.
TENSION: The whole tweetstorm *is* a multi-factor system.
COUNTERARGUMENT: Checklists (aviation, surgery) work by adding variables.

## 7. Build and sell

### NAVAL-018
PRINCIPLE: Learn to sell, learn to build; if you can do both, you will be unstoppable.
SOURCE: Learn to Sell, Learn to Build — https://nav.al/rich
SOURCE CLAIM: Build = product in the industry’s sense. Sell = customers, marketing, recruiting, fundraising, PR. Classic SV: world-class builder + world-class seller.
CONTEXT: Combining skills.
APPLICATION: If we can only do one, name the gap explicitly.
BOUNDARY CONDITIONS: He later says if you had to pick one, start with build then transition to sell; sales reputation can become self-fulfilling.
TENSION: NAVAL-039 (if it feels like selling, you’re selling the wrong thing).
COUNTERARGUMENT: Many winning companies are operator-led, not founder-celebrity sellers.

### NAVAL-019
PRINCIPLE: Sell the truth: credibility > tactics; don’t be attached to the outcome.
SOURCE: Sell the Truth — https://nav.al/sell
SOURCE CLAIM: He doesn’t “sell”; he figures things out and conveys them honestly. Humans resist being sold. Top people see through tactics. If it doesn’t resonate, move on. Raise when *his* excitement from fundamentals crosses a threshold—then convey what’s true, don’t exaggerate.
CONTEXT: Accusations of a reality-distortion field; fundraising for Impossible.
APPLICATION: Can we sell this without misrepresentation?
BOUNDARY CONDITIONS: He knows Cialdini; might use it “once in a blue moon” in an email, not as the engine.
TENSION: “Every founder has to lie to every employee” (NAVAL-038) vs sell-the-truth.
COUNTERARGUMENT: Enterprise sales and politics reward persistence he calls “hammering,” which he rejects as laziness/preference.

## 8. Markets / choosing the game

### NAVAL-020
PRINCIPLE: Pick an industry where you can play long-term games with long-term people.
SOURCE: Play Long-term Games With Long-term People — https://nav.al/long-term
SOURCE CLAIM: Compound interest in relationships, money, learning. Iterated prisoner’s dilemma / tit-for-tat needs repeats. Switching industry/location resets trust. Some fields (politics, freshman House) are transient by design.
CONTEXT: Where to work.
APPLICATION: Cost of leaving ScanScam’s network vs entering a new trade-intel game.
BOUNDARY CONDITIONS: One Silicon Valley move can pay so much that people defect anyway. A new industry *might* be the best opportunity—he still warns you start from scratch.
TENSION: “Live in the future” / hop to AI (NAVAL-063) vs don’t reset the network.
COUNTERARGUMENT: Immigrants and switchers often win *because* they leave ossified games.

### NAVAL-021
PRINCIPLE: Long-term games are positive-sum pie-baking; short-term games are pie-cutting.
SOURCE: https://nav.al/long-term
SOURCE CLAIM: Nivi’s formulation; Naval agrees. Exceptions: people who show up with guns for a slice they didn’t bake.
CONTEXT: Who gets rich together.
APPLICATION: Are we making counterparties richer over repeats, or extracting once?
BOUNDARY CONDITIONS: His anti-socialist aside is ideology, not a market test.
TENSION: NAVAL-028 winner-take-all.
COUNTERARGUMENT: Repeated games also enable cartels and corruption (he notes career-politician downside).

### NAVAL-022
PRINCIPLE: The internet lets you scale a niche obsession to a real audience.
SOURCE: The Internet Has Massively Broadened Career Possibilities — https://nav.al/rich
SOURCE CLAIM: Connects everyone. You may not get Facebook-scale; 50,000 passionate people can be enough. Diversity of humans is the creative superpower; village jobs forced conformity.
CONTEXT: Career space after the internet.
APPLICATION: Niche + authenticity can be a business.
BOUNDARY CONDITIONS: 2026: no demand for average apps; aggregators take super-wealth (NAVAL-067).
TENSION: Think big (NAVAL-023) vs niche is enough.
COUNTERARGUMENT: Attention platforms tax the long tail; “50k fans” is harder than 2019.

### NAVAL-023
PRINCIPLE: It takes as much effort to create a small business as a large one—so think big (rationally).
SOURCE: Compounding Relationships — https://nav.al/relationships
SOURCE CLAIM: Elon vs three restaurants: both 80-hour, stressful, years. Outcomes differ. Think big.
CONTEXT: Compounding; Sam Altman “footnote” projects.
APPLICATION: Don’t spend founder-life on a small pie if a larger one is available at similar sweat.
BOUNDARY CONDITIONS: “Rationally, within your means.”
TENSION: Small experiments / sprints (NAVAL-034); Kelly/ruin (NAVAL-047).
COUNTERARGUMENT: Small businesses can be higher-probability and higher-freedom; “think big” is VC selection.

## 9. Long-term games

### NAVAL-024
PRINCIPLE: All returns in life—wealth, relationships, knowledge—come from compound interest in iterated games.
SOURCE: https://nav.al/long-term
SOURCE CLAIM: 20% for 30 years is not 20×30. Rewards usually at the end. Marriage/kids as compounding. Fitness: easier to stay fit than claw back.
CONTEXT: Why duration matters.
APPLICATION: What compounds if we stay? What resets if we leave?
BOUNDARY CONDITIONS: Compounding needs a stable platform (Buffett + U.S. market not seized).
TENSION: Discrete lump paydays (NAVAL-036) vs smooth compounding.
COUNTERARGUMENT: Many returns are step-functions (one startup win), which he also celebrates.

### NAVAL-025
PRINCIPLE: Apply specific knowledge with leverage and eventually you will get what you deserve—but it can take 10–20 years, and you only have to be right once.
SOURCE: Eventually You Will Get What You Deserve — https://nav.al/rich
SOURCE CLAIM: If you count, you run out of patience. Immediate doesn’t work; the world is efficient. People he tagged brilliant/hardworking two decades ago “are all successful now, almost without exception.” Entrepreneurship: many shots, one hit.
CONTEXT: Timescale.
APPLICATION: Separate “no signal yet” from “wrong game.”
BOUNDARY CONDITIONS: For every winner, multiple failures. Five-year friend success “can drive you insane.”
TENSION: Impatience with actions (NAVAL-035).
COUNTERARGUMENT: Survivorship; lots of brilliant hard workers do not get paid.

### NAVAL-026
PRINCIPLE: There are no get-rich-quick schemes; that’s someone else getting rich off you.
SOURCE: https://nav.al/rich
SOURCE CLAIM: Easy paths are exploited. Selling $79.95 courses as the path is suspect. He won’t put ads on the podcast because it would destroy credibility. Learn fitness from the fit; wealth from people who made it elsewhere, not from selling “how to get rich.”
CONTEXT: Gurus.
APPLICATION: Distrust opportunities whose business model is teaching the opportunity.
BOUNDARY CONDITIONS: Actionable advice that admits difficulty can still be valid.
TENSION: He *is* a high-leverage media teacher of wealth principles.
COUNTERARGUMENT: Teaching can be honest productized knowledge (his own podcast).

## 10. Trust / reputation / relationships

### NAVAL-027
PRINCIPLE: Compounding relationships beat many shallow ones; trust collapses legal friction.
SOURCE: https://nav.al/relationships
SOURCE CLAIM: 20-year mutual trust: maybe no contracts. Founders falling apart is an under-recognized startup killer. Deep > many shallow. Most compounding benefits arrive late.
CONTEXT: Compound interest examples.
APPLICATION: Few high-trust partners vs a wide cold network.
BOUNDARY CONDITIONS: Handshake trust fails in low-trust or high-stakes one-shot deals (contractors—NAVAL-046).
TENSION: Accountability under your own name (public) vs private high-trust pods.
COUNTERARGUMENT: Institutions exist because personal trust doesn’t scale.

### NAVAL-028
PRINCIPLE: Pick partners with intelligence, energy, and integrity—no compromises. Integrity is what they do.
SOURCE: https://nav.al/rich
SOURCE CLAIM: Smart+lazy or energetic+dumb fails. Integrity most important or you have a hardworking crook. Signals over words; waiter test; self-esteem as reputation with yourself. People who *announce* their ethics are often con men. Irrationally ethical example: founder who recapitalized investors through pivots “for me, not for you.”
CONTEXT: Who to work with.
APPLICATION: Watch behavior under no audience.
BOUNDARY CONDITIONS: “Smart” is domain-specific, not a slur.
TENSION: Don’t partner with pessimists (NAVAL-029) vs judgment requiring seeing pitfalls.
COUNTERARGUMENT: Integrity theater is common; private life is noisy data.

### NAVAL-029
PRINCIPLE: Don’t partner with cynics and pessimists; their beliefs are self-fulfilling. Be a rational optimist with an action bias.
SOURCE: Partner With Rational Optimists — https://nav.al/rich
SOURCE CLAIM: Create things → need optimism about capability plus seeing the world as it is. Pure critics who won’t lead/follow/get out of the way block creation. We’re descended from pessimists; modern upside is fat-tailed; avoid ruin (jail, body, total capital loss). BOCTAOE.
CONTEXT: Who not to hire.
APPLICATION: “Helpful critical guy” is a no unless they also propose.
BOUNDARY CONDITIONS: Foolhardy optimism chasing unworthy things is “nothing worse.”
TENSION: Peace/desire reduction vs “why not try to build something big.”
COUNTERARGUMENT: Pessimists prevent blow-ups; “unlimited upside, limited downside” is not true outside VC.

## 11. Negotiation

### NAVAL-030
PRINCIPLE: Negotiations are won by whoever cares less.
SOURCE: Turn Short-Term Games Into Long-Term Games — https://nav.al/short
SOURCE CLAIM: If you want it too badly, they extract more. Desire is leverage against you.
CONTEXT: Dealmaking.
APPLICATION: Walk-away as the actual power.
BOUNDARY CONDITIONS: Caring less can mean you lose deals you should have taken.
TENSION: Intrinsic obsession (you *do* care) vs negotiating as if you don’t.
COUNTERARGUMENT: Repeated high-care advocacy wins in sales and politics.

### NAVAL-031
PRINCIPLE: If you’re being extracted, convert a one-shot game into a repeated, reputation-bearing game.
SOURCE: https://nav.al/short
SOURCE CLAIM: Contractors/homeowners: low information, high cost, cheating both ways. Fixes: friends, reputation, second project contingent on first, friends waiting, public reviews. Pareto superior always take; Pareto optimal means hard tradeoffs.
CONTEXT: House renovation as single-move game.
APPLICATION: Add future games, witnesses, or reviews when leverage is poor.
BOUNDARY CONDITIONS: Some games cannot be iterated (one house, one life).
TENSION: NAVAL-019 walk away vs converting the game.
COUNTERARGUMENT: Reputation systems are gamed; reviews aren’t trust.

### NAVAL-032
PRINCIPLE: Prefer walking away from shackling bad deals; compromise is the enemy of a great business.
SOURCE: Good Deal or No Deal — https://nav.al/sell
SOURCE CLAIM: Contracts constrain future optionality to hunt a stag together. Bad investors/board/vetoes are hard to unwind. Plan so your back isn’t against the wall (raise 6–12 months early). Gut sinking = don’t do it. Be pragmatic, but lean walk-away.
CONTEXT: Dealmaking.
APPLICATION: Optionality vs getting it done.
BOUNDARY CONDITIONS: Easy-to-unwind deals can be more forgiving.
TENSION: Action bias / solve tonight (NAVAL-035).
COUNTERARGUMENT: Perfect deals don’t close; holdouts die.

## 12. Reading / learning / epistemology

### NAVAL-033
PRINCIPLE: There is no skill called business. Read what you love until you love to read; foundations over magazines.
SOURCE: Read What You Love… — https://nav.al/rich
SOURCE CLAIM: Avoid business magazines/classes; study microeconomics, game theory, psychology, persuasion, ethics, math, computers. Reading is the foundation. Original scientific books (Smith, Darwin, Feynman) program later judgment. Means of learning are abundant; desire is scarce. “Reading is faster than listening, doing is faster than watching.”
CONTEXT: Continuous learning tweets.
APPLICATION: Treat guru content as entertainment unless it points to foundations + doing.
BOUNDARY CONDITIONS: Start wherever you are (even fiction) to build the habit.
TENSION: Doing faster than watching vs 2019 podcast as leverage.
COUNTERARGUMENT: Domain journals and living practitioners beat 1776 texts for operational work.

### NAVAL-034
PRINCIPLE: Advice is usually someone’s winning lottery numbers; listen to everyone, ignore everyone, keep your own model.
SOURCE: Reject Most Advice — https://nav.al/rich
SOURCE CLAIM: Best founders survey then discard. If you survey enough, advice cancels to zero. Tweets are hooks to *your* experience, not posters for people without the experience. If too much of this podcast feels untrue, delete it.
CONTEXT: Copying paths.
APPLICATION: Map maxims onto *this* situation; don’t import 2018 Naval as law.
BOUNDARY CONDITIONS: There is something to learn; just not the exact circumstance.
TENSION: This entire library is advice.
COUNTERARGUMENT: Apprenticeship is taking someone’s numbers on purpose.

## 13. Decision-making

### NAVAL-035
PRINCIPLE: Impatience with actions, patience with results.
SOURCE: Work As Hard As You Can — https://nav.al/rich
SOURCE CLAIM: Do the thing now; markets and products take time. He won’t sleep until a problem is at least in motion.
CONTEXT: Patience paradox.
APPLICATION: Speed of first motion ≠ speed of payoff.
BOUNDARY CONDITIONS: Personal “won’t sleep” may be temperament, not a universal.
TENSION: Calm mind (NAVAL-054) vs no peace until solved.
COUNTERARGUMENT: Sleep-deprived “fix it tonight” is how people destroy judgment.

### NAVAL-036
PRINCIPLE: Inspiration is perishable; work in sprints, not fake 120-hour weeks.
SOURCE: https://nav.al/rich
SOURCE CLAIM: Nobody really works 80–120 hours at high output. Knowledge work: lion, not marathoner. Seize inspiration (tweetstorms). Product-market-*founder* fit > raw hours. Who and what > how hard, but if wealth is the goal you still work as hard as you can.
CONTEXT: Twitter work-ethic wars (DHH vs Rabois).
APPLICATION: Don’t status-signal hours; design recovery.
BOUNDARY CONDITIONS: Olympics-of-startups vs lifestyle business are different games—he says they’re talking past each other.
TENSION: Wealth as number-one priority (NAVAL-037).
COUNTERARGUMENT: Some domains (surgery, manufacturing scale-up) don’t allow lion schedules.

### NAVAL-037
PRINCIPLE: Set an absurd aspirational hourly rate; you can’t penny-pinch your way to wealth.
SOURCE: Set an Aspirational Hourly Rate — https://nav.al/rich
SOURCE CLAIM: Value time in every decision. His number: $5,000/hr even before money. Outsource below the rate. Paul Graham: product-market fit + health, that’s all you have time for. If you optimize lifestyle joy, you’re not on the wealth mission.
CONTEXT: Valuing time.
APPLICATION: Meeting/errand tax vs judgment work.
BOUNDARY CONDITIONS: Penny-pinching *can* get basic sustenance / early retirement—he calls that valid, just not this project.
TENSION: Peace/happiness vs wealth-as-top-priority.
COUNTERARGUMENT: Rate is circular (you’re not actually paid $5k/hr); it can justify neglect.

### NAVAL-038
PRINCIPLE: A busy calendar and a busy mind destroy great work. Be too busy to “do coffee,” keep an uncluttered calendar, demand proof of work.
SOURCE: Be Too Busy to ‘Do Coffee’ — https://nav.al/rich
SOURCE CLAIM: Ruthlessly decline non-transactional meetings. Walking/standing/small. Product progress is the unfakeable resume. Early-career networking ≠ later exploitation phase. Free time and free mind required.
CONTEXT: Calendar as death.
APPLICATION: Coffee as a default no.
BOUNDARY CONDITIONS: Early career: exploring/meetings more OK.
TENSION: Compounding relationships need *some* unstructured time.
COUNTERARGUMENT: Many deals still start as coffee.

## 14. Time and freedom

## Naval in 2026

**FACT:** The Feb 2026 episodes (`https://nav.al/ai`, `https://nav.al/code`, `https://nav.al/industrial`) are a different register than the 2018–2019 wealth interviews: more building, agents, verification, aggregators, regulation.

### What has changed? (sourced, not inferred as his autobiography)
- **How you command leverage:** 2019: learn to code to order robot armies. 2026: specify in English; agents code; *training models* as the new programming elite; software engineers still win at leaky abstractions and novel domains.
- **Who can ship:** “Anyone can now make an application” + tsunami of slop; medium software firms at risk; aggregators capture super-wealth (`https://nav.al/ai`).
- **His allocation of time:** Returned to a company (Impossible) because talking without market/physics feedback becomes armchair philosophy (`https://nav.al/ai`).
- **Moat anxiety:** Open question whether *pure software* remains a company-shaped game (`https://nav.al/industrial`).
- **Labor inside the firm:** Humans as verifiers signing off consequences (`https://nav.al/industrial`).

### What remains consistent?
- Best-in-the-world / redefine the niche (`https://nav.al/ai` explicitly says the old tweet still applies).
- Judgment, taste, accountability, long-term games, permissionless media, no demand for being average.
- Wealth as assets + freedom; specific knowledge as whatever isn’t yet stamped out—now including *taste + verification* as the unstamped layer.
- Live-in-the-future as **using** the tech, not forecasting punditry.

### How does AI alter leverage?
He describes it as more leverage on judgment: waste tokens to save human time; always take the smarter model when you’ll pour capital/code/people/marketing behind the answer (`https://nav.al/industrial`). Agency of agents reduces the need to spend twenty years on jargon before contributing—**if** you bring taste (`https://nav.al/industrial` discussion). Code leverage becomes cheaper; **decision** leverage gets more expensive.

### What becomes scarce if intelligence becomes cheap?
From his 2026 talk, not from a slogan: **taste, creativity, clear desire/vision, verification, being #1 in a niche, aggregator position, hardware/atoms, model-training skill, and aligned (or at least accountable) humans using the tools.** He does **not** publish a complete scarcity list. Do not add “trust” or “ScanScam” as his words.

### What does “live in the future” mean operationally?
Sourced 2026: be an avid consumer of the new stack so you can recognize and use it; action against AI anxiety; build with agents rather than commentate (`https://nav.al/ai`). Not sourced from the `/future` index page.

### What kinds of opportunity would he likely reject even if profitable? (only where he has said the shape)
- Status-primary games (`https://nav.al/seek-wealth`)
- Get-rich-quick / selling the dream of getting rich (`https://nav.al/rich`)
- Renting time with matched inputs/outputs as the *plan* (`https://nav.al/rich`)
- Ruinous: illegal, integrity wipe, bet-the-kitty (`https://nav.al/rich`)
- Selling what you don’t believe; feeling like a salesman (`https://nav.al/sell`)
- Average products in winner-take-all software (`https://nav.al/ai`)
- Cynic-run teams (`https://nav.al/rich`)
- One-shot high-cheat games you cannot convert to reputation (`https://nav.al/short`)

He does **not** say he would reject slow, local, or non-tech wealth; he argues they are harder paths to *his* definition of leveraged wealth.
